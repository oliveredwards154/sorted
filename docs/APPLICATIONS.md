## Describe your website (short, ~250 characters)

Sorted. is a UK menswear site that takes the choice out of getting dressed. Men pick an occasion (wedding, interview, date, pub, office, weekend), choose between a few looks, and we link them to buy each piece at three price levels.

## Describe your website (long)

Sorted. helps men who don't enjoy shopping dress well for a specific occasion. A visitor picks where they're going, chooses between two outfits three times, and we build a complete look from their choices: outer layer, top, bottoms and shoes, with options from UK shops at under £40, £40–£100 and £100+. Visitors can also upload a photo of something they own and we suggest similar pieces.

Every product link goes to the retailer's own product page. We show prices, stock and images from partner product feeds and refresh them daily. Our audience is UK men aged roughly 20–45 with a clear reason to buy (an upcoming wedding, interview or date), so clicks carry strong purchase intent.

## Payment details

Networks pay into one bank account. While you're a sole trader, use a separate sole-trader account in the account holder's name (Monzo, Starling and others offer free ones). Don't paste bank details into the launch or setup trackers.

## Order to sign up

1. **Domain and email** first. Every network checks the site at its own address.
2. **Skimlinks** (or Sovrn Commerce, not both). One approval covers thousands of shops, so every shop link earns from day one. Free; they keep about a quarter of the commission.
3. **Awin**. £5 deposit, refunded with the first payout. Then apply to brands from the launch hub.
4. Rakuten, CJ, Impact, Webgains as you need them (free).
