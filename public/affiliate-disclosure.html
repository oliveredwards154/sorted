How we make money

Affiliate disclosure

Sorted. is free to use. We earn money through affiliate links.

What that means

When you click "Shop" and buy something from a partner shop, that shop may pay us a small commission on the sale. You pay the same price as you would going to the shop directly.

Shop links on Sorted. are marked as sponsored, and they go through our own address (sorted…/go/…) so we can tell the shop the sale came from us.

How we choose what to show

Looks are chosen for the occasion first. We show products from shops whether or not they pay us, and commission never decides which look you see.

Networks we work with

We work with affiliate networks such as Awin, Rakuten Advertising, CJ, Impact, Partnerize and Webgains, and directly with some independent brands. These networks may set their own cookies on the shop's website when you click through, to track the sale. See their privacy policies for details.

We earn a commission when you buy through some links on this site. It never changes the price you pay. How this works
