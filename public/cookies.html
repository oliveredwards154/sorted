Cookies and storage

Cookies

Sorted. doesn't set any cookies. No advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, so there's no banner to click.

What we store

  • Local storage: your wardrobe (saved styles, finds and uploaded photos) is kept in your browser so you can come back to it. This is part of a feature you choose to use, so it doesn't need a consent banner. Clear it from the Wardrobe page or your browser settings.
  • Session storage: remembers your picks in the this-or-that rounds while the tab is open.

How we count visits without cookies

When you view a page or use a feature, your browser tells our server, for example, "someone started the wedding rounds". We add one to that day's total. Nothing is stored on your device for this, and nothing identifies you or links your visits together. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, it isn't sent at all.

When you go to a shop

When you click a shop link, the affiliate network and the shop may set cookies on their own websites to track the sale. Those cookies belong to them, and their own cookie notices apply.

Note for the team: if you ever add a third-party analytics or advertising tool that sets cookies or reads from the device, update this page and add a consent banner first.
