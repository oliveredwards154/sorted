What we collect

  • Photos you upload. When you upload a photo of clothing, we send it to our AI provider (Anthropic) once, to work out what the item is and its colour. We don't store the photo on our servers. Anthropic processes it under its commercial API terms, which don't use API data to train its models by default.
  • Your wardrobe. Saved styles, finds and the small copies of photos you upload are stored in your own browser (local storage). They never reach our server. Clear them any time from the Wardrobe page.
  • Clicks to shops. When you click through to a shop we log the time, the product or shop, the occasion and a random reference, so we can match sales reports from affiliate networks. We don't log your name or email.
  • Server logs. Our host keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security, for a short period.

Why we use it (lawful basis)

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interests in running and improving the site and being paid by partner shops, and, for photo recognition, to provide the feature you asked for.
