About

Sorted. takes the choice out of menswear. Tell us where you're going, pick between a few looks, and we put the outfit together with pieces you can buy straight away.

How it works

Pick an occasion: the pub, a wedding, a job interview, a first date, the office or a casual weekend. We show you two looks at a time for three rounds. Your picks become one style, with a couple of options for each part of the outfit, from UK shops at three price levels.

Already own something you like? Upload a photo and we'll find similar pieces.

Who we are

Sorted. is an independent UK project. We're not owned by any shop or brand, and we pick looks on what works, not on who pays the most commission.
